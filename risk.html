Risk warning

Demo accounts

Demo accounts use virtual funds and simulated prices. Results on a demo account do not guarantee results on a real account.

Responsible trading

  • Set daily loss and turnover limits in Settings.
  • Use self-exclusion if you need a break.
  • Seek help if trading is affecting your wellbeing.

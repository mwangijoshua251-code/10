Trade types

Eight ways to trade, each with risk fully defined before you enter.

Rise / Fall

Predict whether the exit spot at the end of your duration is strictly higher (Rise) or lower (Fall) than the entry spot. Durations from 1 tick (derived markets) to 30 days. A tie is a loss.

Higher / Lower

Choose a barrier offset from the current spot. Higher wins if the exit spot finishes above the barrier; Lower wins if it finishes below. The further the barrier, the bigger the payout.

Touch / No Touch

Touch wins the moment the market reaches your barrier at any time before expiry. No Touch wins if it never does.

Matches / Differs

Pick a digit 0–9. Win if the last digit of the exit spot matches (≈10% chance, large payout) or differs (≈90% chance, small payout). Derived markets only.

Even / Odd

Predict whether the last digit of the exit spot is even or odd.

Over / Under

Predict whether the last digit is over or under your chosen digit.

Accumulators

Your stake grows by a fixed rate (1%–5%) for every tick the spot stays inside a barrier range around the previous tick. Cash out any time; if the market breaks the barrier you lose your stake. Optional take profit.

Multipliers

Magnify price moves by ×20 to ×400 (derived) or ×5 to ×100 (other markets). Your loss can never exceed your stake. Add take profit and stop loss, and cancel any time. A small commission applies.
